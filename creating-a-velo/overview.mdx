Velo offers five ways to make a video, so you can start from whatever you already have — a live screen, an existing recording, a document, a URL, or nothing but a prompt. In each case Velo reasons through the source, generates the script, and renders the finished video.

</Frame>

The home screen groups these into three tabs. **Start from scratch** holds two cards — *Start with a screen recording* and *Start with a prompt*. **Upload Video/PDF** takes an existing file. **Paste a link** hands a URL to the browser agent.

## Direct screen capture

The **Capture screen** option is designed for users who need to record a specific tab, window, or their entire desktop on the fly. This method uses the Velo browser extension to facilitate the recording process. It's the preferred choice when you don't have a pre-existing video file and want to begin automated processing immediately after finishing a live demonstration.

## Uploading Recordings and Documents

For users who already have produced content, **Upload a recording or PDF** takes an existing file straight into processing. This method supports standard video formats including MP4 and MOV.

Beyond video, this flow also supports uploading a PDF — specifically intended for sharing explanations of static documents such as pitch decks, PRDs, or project explainers. When a document is uploaded, the platform asks a few brief questions to help structure the resulting video message around the file's content.

## Link-Based Assisted Recording

**Paste a Link** turns any live website or web app into a narrated video. Paste the URL, walk through it once while narrating, and Velo's browser agent takes care of the rest. It navigates your product and records the final video on its own, exactly as you scripted it.

<Tip>
  Don't have an asset to start from — just something you need to explain? [Prompt to Video](/prompt-to-video/overview) generates a video directly from a text prompt, no recording or upload required.
</Tip>
