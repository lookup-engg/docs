title: "Video Analytics"
description: "Track views, unique viewers, watch time, and reactions for every video you've created in Velo."
---

Every video you create in Velo comes with built-in analytics, so you can see how it's performing after it's shared — no separate tool required.

## Finding Analytics

**Step 1:** Go to **My Videos** in the left sidebar to see every video in your workspace.

<Frame>
  ![My Videos list](/images/creating-a-velo/video-analytics/01.png)
</Frame>

**Step 2:** Each video card shows an **Analytics** link in the bottom-right corner. Click it to open the analytics panel for that video.

## What You Can See

The **Video analytics** modal shows:

- **Views** — total number of times the video has been played
- **Unique Viewers** — the number of distinct people who watched
- **Total Watched** — cumulative watch time across all viewers
- **Total Reactions** — total emoji reactions left on the share page
- A per-viewer breakdown once people start watching (name/email if known, when they watched, and how much of the video they viewed)

<Frame>
  ![Video analytics modal](/images/creating-a-velo/video-analytics/02.png)
</Frame>

Until someone opens the share link, the panel shows all metrics at zero with a placeholder: *"No viewers yet. Once people watch this video, they'll show up here."*

<Note>
  Some stats may be delayed slightly — the panel includes a refresh indicator if data is still catching up.
</Note>

## Notes

- Analytics are tracked per rendered video and reset if you re-render — the card shows the current render's timestamp (e.g. "Rendered Aug 4, 2026 • 5:32 AM").
- Views and reactions are counted from the public [share page](/creating-a-velo/sharing), not from teammates viewing the video inside Velo itself.
