## How it works

- **One recording:** You read a script of about **60** words. Velo builds your voice clone from it.
- **You name it:** You pick your clone by its name, in the voice picker or in a prompt.
- **Use it anywhere:** Your clone appears with the public voices whenever you choose a narrator.

## Before you start

- A quiet room and a working microphone.
- A Pro or Ultra plan. On Free, the voice page asks you to upgrade.

## Create your voice clone

1. In the sidebar, select **Voice clone**.
2. Select **+ Create new**.
3. Enter a name for your clone (for example, Vanessa).
4. Choose a cloning style: **Classic** or **Performance**.
5. Choose your microphone.
6. Select **Start recording** and read the script on screen.
7. Select **Finish recording**.

Velo cleans up the audio and builds your clone. This can take a moment. When it's ready, it appears under **Your Voices**, and you can play a preview.

If you see **Couldn't capture your voice**, select **Re-record**. Background noise or skipped words usually cause it.

<Tip>
Speak at your normal pace, as you would to a colleague. A relaxed read makes the clone sound more like you.
</Tip>

## Cloning styles

- **Classic:** The default. Works for most narration.
- **Performance:** More expressive. English only. Each time it speaks, your recording and its transcript are sent to an external voice service.

## Manage your clone

- **Share with workspace:** Let everyone in your workspace use your voice. See [Share clones with your workspace](/voices-and-avatars/share-clones-with-your-workspace).
- **Delete:** Removes the clone. Videos you've already made keep their narration.

## FAQs

**Can I use my voice clone in a prompt?**

Yes. Name it in your request (for example, "Narrate with my voice Vanessa"). Velo uses the name you gave it.

**Why is my clone saying a word wrong?**

Add the word in **Brand Kit** → **Pronunciations**. Every new narration uses it, including voice clones. See [Brand Kit](/organize/brand-kit#set-up-pronunciation).

**Can my teammates use my voice clone?**

Yes, once you share it with your workspace. See [Share clones with your workspace](/voices-and-avatars/share-clones-with-your-workspace).
