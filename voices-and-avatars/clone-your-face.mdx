## How it works

- **A photo and a short clip:** Velo uses a photo of you and a short reference clip to learn how you look and move.
- **You name it:** You pick your clone by its name, in the avatar picker or in a prompt.
- **Pair it with your voice:** Use it with your voice clone so the presenter looks and sounds like you.

## Before you start

- A camera, and permission for your browser to use it.
- Good, even light on your face, and a plain background.
- A Pro or Ultra plan. On Free, the face page asks you to upgrade.

## Create your face clone

1. In the sidebar, select **Face clone**.
2. Select **Create face clone**.
3. In **Name your face clone**, enter a name (for example, Vanessa).
4. Select **Click picture**.
5. Allow camera access when your browser asks.
6. Look straight at the camera and wait for the countdown.
7. Check the preview, then select **Continue**. To try again, select **Retake**.

When it's ready, your face clone appears under your custom faces.

<Tip>
Face a window or a lamp rather than sitting with light behind you. Even light gives the clone a clearer face.
</Tip>

## Manage your clone

- **Share with workspace:** Let everyone in your workspace present with your face. See [Share clones with your workspace](/voices-and-avatars/share-clones-with-your-workspace).
- **Delete:** Removes the clone.

## FAQs

**Can I use my face clone in a prompt?**

Yes. Name it in your request (for example, "Add my avatar Vanessa in the bottom right corner").

**Do I need to film myself for every video?**

No. Once your face clone is ready, Velo presents for you. To film yourself instead, use [Record Yourself](/create/record-yourself).

**What if my clone looks off?**

Delete it and create a new one in better light, looking straight at the camera.
