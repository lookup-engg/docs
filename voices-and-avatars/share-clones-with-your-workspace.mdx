## Share a clone

1. In the sidebar, select **Voice clone** or **Face clone**.
2. Find your clone and open its menu.
3. Select **Share with workspace**.

What happens next: the clone appears under **Workspace Wide** for everyone in your workspace.

## Delete a clone

1. Open the clone's menu.
2. Select **Delete** and confirm.

<Warning>
Deleting a clone is **permanent**. Your teammates can't use it anymore either. Videos already made with it keep their narration and presenter.
</Warning>

## FAQs

**Can I stop sharing a clone?**

  Open the clone's menu and change its sharing.

**Can teammates edit my shared clone?**

No. They can use it in their videos.

**Can Velo share a clone from Slack or Claude?**

Velo MCP can share clones. Creating clones happens in the web app.
