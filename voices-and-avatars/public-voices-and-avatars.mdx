**Plans:** Free, Pro, Ultra · **Uses credits:** No (choosing); Yes (when Velo narrates or generates)

Pick a ready-made voice and face for your videos, with nothing to record.

> *Placeholder (gif): Voice clone in the sidebar with Public Voices highlighted, playing two voice samples, then Face clone with the public faces*

## Where to find them

- **Voices:** In the sidebar, select **Voice clone**. The voice page lists your voices, voices shared with your workspace, and public voices.
- **Faces:** In the sidebar, select **Face clone**. The face page lists your faces, faces shared with your workspace, and public faces.
- **While you make a video:** The voice and face pickers in **Generate with Avatar** and **Change voice** show the same lists.

Velo has **15** public voices and **6** public faces.

## Choose a voice or face

1. Open the voice or face list.
2. Play a voice sample, or look at the faces.
3. Pick one in the picker, or name it in your request (for example, "Narrate with Drew").

## Good to know

- **Every plan:** Public voices and faces work on Free, Pro and Ultra.
- **Your own instead:** To sound or look like yourself, create a clone. See [Clone your voice](/voices-and-avatars/clone-your-voice).
- **Pronunciation:** Pronunciation entries in your Brand Kit apply to public voices too.

## FAQs

**Can I use a public avatar on the Free plan?**

Yes. On Free, you can use one avatar face per billing cycle.

**Can I change the voice after a video is made?**

Yes. Use the **Change voice** quick action. See [Quick actions](/edit/quick-actions).

**Can I hear a voice before I pick it?**

Yes. Play its sample in the voice list.
