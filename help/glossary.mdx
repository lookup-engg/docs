Quick definitions of the words you'll see in Velo.

- **Agent:** The Velo Agent, which asks questions, writes the script and builds and edits your video. See [How the Velo Agent works](/get-started/how-the-velo-agent-works).
- **Asset:** A file in your workspace's shared Asset Library that Velo can use in videos. See [Asset Library](/organize/assets).
- **Brand Kit:** Your workspace's logo, fonts, colors, tone, intro/outro and pronunciations, applied to every new video. See [Brand Kit](/organize/brand-kit).
- **Agentic Recording:** The part of Velo that explores a website and records it. See [Turn a URL into a video](/create/turn-a-url-into-a-video).
- **Chat:** Where each video starts and where you edit it. Listed in Recent chats.
- **Clone:** Your own voice or face, created in Velo. See [Clone your voice](/voices-and-avatars/clone-your-voice) and [Clone your face](/voices-and-avatars/clone-your-face).
- **Connector:** A link to another tool, such as a knowledge base, Slack or Velo MCP. See [Connectors overview](/connect/connectors-overview).
- **Credits:** The single balance your workspace uses for everything Velo makes. See [Plans and pricing](/plans-and-account/plans-and-pricing).
- **Knowledge base:** A crawled copy of your website or docs that Velo uses for context. See [Knowledge Bases](/connect/knowledge-bases).
- **Library:** A group of videos. Every video lives in General. See [Libraries](/organize/libraries).
- **Pronunciation:** A Brand Kit entry that tells narration how to say a word. See [Brand Kit](/organize/brand-kit#set-up-pronunciation).
- **Public avatar:** A ready-made face or voice anyone can use. See [Public voices and avatars](/voices-and-avatars/public-voices-and-avatars).
- **Quick action:** A one-click change in the chat, such as **Change voice**. See [Quick actions](/edit/quick-actions).
- **Render:** Turning your latest version into a finished video file or share link.
- **Template:** A reusable video format from Velo or your team. See [Templates](/organize/templates).
- **Version:** A saved state of your video after each edit. See [Version history](/edit/version-history).
- **Workspace:** Where your team's videos, Brand Kit, members and credits live. See [Workspace, members and roles](/plans-and-account/workspace-members-and-roles).
