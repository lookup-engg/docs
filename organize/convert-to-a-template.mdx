## Before you start

- Export the video first. The template then gets a sample video and thumbnail.

## Make a template

1. Open the video in the AI Editor and select **Convert to a template**. You can also use the card menu in **Libraries**, or ask in chat.
2. Add instructions, if you like (for example, "Keep the dark look and quick cuts, but leave out our logo"). Up to **800** characters.
3. Velo drafts the template and shows a review card.
4. Pick a name, or enter your own.
5. Untick anything you don't want to keep.
6. Pick up to **5** categories.
7. Choose whether to keep the voice, avatar and intro/outro.
8. Save it.

What happens next: the template shows up for everyone in your workspace, in the gallery and by name in chat.

## What a template keeps

- **The look:** Colors, fonts, layout, motion and transitions.
- **Building blocks:** Title cards, lower thirds and other reusable pieces.
- **Style rules from the chat:** Instructions you gave in the video's chat, such as a pronunciation fix or "no zooms".
- **What it leaves out:** The video's own words, numbers, screenshots, recordings and presenter.

## FAQs

**Why doesn't my template have a sample video?**

The video wasn't exported before you converted it, or the export was older than your last edits. Export it and convert again.

**Will my Brand Kit still apply?**

Yes. Your Brand Kit applies on top of the template.

**Can I edit a template after saving it?**

You can rename or delete it. To change its look, convert an updated video.
