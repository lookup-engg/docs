## How it works

- **Velo learns from your website:** Velo reads your site and picks up your logo, colors, fonts, tone of voice and positioning. It also designs an intro and outro card for you.
- **Every new video uses it:** You don't need to mention your brand in a prompt.
- **Velo checks before going off-brand:** If a prompt asks for something your kit doesn't cover or contradicts, Velo asks first.
- **Existing videos don't change:** Edits to your kit apply to new videos only.

## Create your Brand Kit

1. In the sidebar, select **Brand Kit**.
2. Select **Create from website**.
3. In **Enter your website**, enter your company's homepage (for example, yourcompany.com).
4. Select **Create**.

Building a new kit takes a few minutes. You can leave the page while Velo works.

If your workspace already has a kit, Velo reuses it, so it's ready right away. When you see **Your Brand Kit is ready**, your kit has **7** tabs, including **6** sample videos.

<Note>
Use your main public homepage. Landing pages and sign-in pages give Velo less to work with.
</Note>

## Brand Kit contents

Velo fills in every tab except Pronunciations from your website. You can edit any of them.

- **Overview:** Your tagline, logos, tone of voice and positioning. Tone shapes how Velo writes every script and on-screen line.
- **Fonts:** Typefaces for **Heading**, **Sub-Heading**, **Title**, **Body** and **Captions**. If your brand font isn't listed, upload the font file (up to **2 MB**).
- **Colors:** Your primary, secondary, accent and background colors.  Add a usage note to any color (for example, "accent for buttons only"), and Velo follows it.
- **Intro/Outro:** The branded opening and closing scenes Velo designs for you, or your own clips (see [Intro/Outro](#intro-outro)).
- **Aesthetics:** The visual motifs and motion style Velo uses in your videos.
- **Pronunciations:** How narration should say brand names and other terms (see below).
- **Samples:** **6** sample videos made with your kit (Intro, Outro, Explainer, Why us, Feature spotlight and How it works), so you can check the result.

## Intro/Outro

Velo designs a branded intro and outro card for your workspace. You can add them to every new video, or upload your own clips.

1. In **Brand Kit**, open the **Intro/Outro** tab.
2. Turn on **Use Intro/Outro card in all videos** to add both cards to every new video.
3. To use your own clip instead, select **Upload your own** under **Intro** or **Outro**.
4. Choose an H.264 MP4 or MOV file, up to **30** seconds long.

With the switch off, you can still add or remove the intro and outro in each video. One switch covers both cards.

## Set up pronunciation

Pronunciation entries tell Velo how narration should say brand names, product names, acronyms and people's names. Narration uses the pronunciation, while scripts, subtitles and on-screen text keep the standard spelling.

1. In **Brand Kit**, open the **Pronunciations** tab.
2. Select **+ Add term**.
3. In **Word or phrase**, enter the word as it's written (for example, velo).
4. In **How should it sound?**, describe how to say it in plain words (for example, hello with a v). Explain it the way you would to a colleague.
5. Select **Generate voice**. This can take up to a minute.
6. Play the generated voice to hear how Velo says it. To hear it in another voice, choose one from the voice menu.
7. Select **Save pronunciation**.

You need to generate the voice before you can save. For example, with **velo** saved as "hello with a v", narration says "vello" and the subtitles still show "Velo".

Add as many words and phrases as you need. Each one gets its own entry in the list. Choose an output voice to hear how each one sounds.

If it doesn't sound right, change **How should it sound?** and select **Regenerate voice**. Once you save it, every new narration in your workspace uses it, including AI-generated and cloned voices.

- **How to describe it:** Plain words work best (for example, "rhymes with hello, starts with a v"). Velo works out the rest.
- **Matching:** Entries match whole words and ignore case unless you turn on **Match case**.  Plurals and dotted forms (for example, Velo.ai) need their own entries.
- **Limits:** Up to **500** entries per workspace.  Entries apply to new narration only, so existing videos aren't re-voiced.
- **Access:** Members can view the list. Admins can edit it.
- **Bulk import:** Import a CSV to add or update many entries at once. Imports never delete entries, and you can review changes before they apply.

## Rebuild your Brand Kit

If your brand changes, Velo can rebuild your kit from your website.

- **Regenerate:** Velo reads your website again, rebuilds the kit and remakes the **6** sample videos. Available once your sample videos finish.
- **Create from website:** Velo creates the kit from the website you enter and reuses your existing kit when it can. Links you've already shared keep showing the brand they were sent with.

<Warning>
Both options **replace** your manual edits.  Copy down any changes you want to keep before you start.
</Warning>

## Team access

- **One kit per workspace:** Everyone in your workspace shares the same Brand Kit.
- **Who can edit:** Admins and owners can edit the Brand Kit. Everyone else's videos still use it.
- **Sharing:** Select **Copy link** to get a page you can send to designers or agencies.

## FAQs

**Can I use a different look for one video?**

Yes. Ask for it in your prompt (for example, "use a dark background for this video"). Velo asks whether to apply it to this video only or save it to your kit.

**Will my existing videos update when I change my Brand Kit?**

No. To update an older video, open its chat and ask Velo to apply your current Brand Kit.

**Can a workspace have more than one Brand Kit?**

No. Each workspace has one Brand Kit. For a second brand, such as another product or a client, create a separate workspace.

**Can I create a Brand Kit on the Free plan?**

Yes, if your workspace was created with a work email. Workspaces created with a personal email need Pro or Ultra, which your workspace owner can upgrade to.
