## Set up your public library

1. In the sidebar, select **Libraries**.
2. In the header, open **Velo Public Library**.
3. Turn it on.
4. Choose which libraries appear.
5. Choose who can sign in: anyone with an email, people from listed email domains, or listed email addresses.
6. Copy the link and share it.

People you add by email address get an invitation.

## What viewers see

- **Your brand:** The library shows your workspace's name and logo.
- **Sign-in by email code:** Viewers enter their email, then a **6**-digit code. They don't need a Velo account.
- **Videos and playlists:** They browse your libraries and play videos.
- **Comments and reactions:** Viewers can react and comment. Team-only comments stay private.

## Access rules

- **Code expiry:** A code expires after **10** minutes and stops working after **5** wrong tries.
- **Staying signed in:** Viewers stay signed in for **30** days.
- **General library:** Only videos with their own share link turned on can appear.
- **View counts:** Hidden wherever the creator turned them off.

## FAQs

**Is the Velo Public Library open to anyone?**

No. Only the people you allow can sign in: anyone with an email, listed domains or listed addresses.

**Do viewers need a Velo account?**

No. They sign in with a code sent to their email.

**Why doesn't a video from General show up?**

Its share link is off. Turn on sharing for the video. See [Share a link](/share/share-a-link).
