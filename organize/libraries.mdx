## How libraries work

- **General:** Every workspace has a General library, and every video lives there.
- **Your libraries:** Create more libraries to group videos, such as onboarding or release videos.
- **Visibility:** A library is **Workspace** (everyone in the workspace) or **Private** (only you and people you add).

## Create a library

1. In the sidebar, select **Libraries**.
2. Create a new library and name it.
3. Choose **Workspace** or **Private**.
4. Select **Add Video** to add videos.

## Add a video to a library

1. In **My videos**, or in the video's chat, select **Add to library**.
2. Choose one or more libraries.

General always includes the video, so you're adding it to extra libraries.

## Manage a library

- **Edit access:** Change who can open the library.
- **Activity:** See recent changes.
- **Sort and filter:** Order and narrow the videos.
- **Share Library:** Share the library with people in your workspace. To publish it to viewers outside your workspace, use [Velo Public Library](/organize/velo-public-library).

## FAQs

**Can I remove a video from General?**

No. Every video lives in General.

**Who can create libraries?**

Everyone in your workspace.

**How do I share libraries with customers?**

Use the Velo Public Library. See [Velo Public Library](/organize/velo-public-library).
