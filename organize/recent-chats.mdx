Every video starts as a chat. Find, rename, pin or delete your chats from the sidebar.

> *Placeholder (gif): the Recent chats list in the sidebar highlighted , searching, renaming a chat, pinning it, then the Delete confirmation*

## Find and organize chats

- **Search:** Find a chat by name.
- **Rename:** Give a chat a clearer name.
- **Pin:** Keep a chat at the top of the list.

Open a chat's menu to rename or pin it.

## Delete a chat and its video

1. In **Recent chats**, open the chat's menu.
2. Select **Delete**.
3. Confirm.

<Warning>
Deleting a chat also deletes its video. This is **permanent**. Download the video first if you need a copy.
</Warning>

## FAQs

**Is deleting a chat the only way to delete a video?**

Yes. My videos has no Delete, by design.

**Can Velo delete videos from Slack or Claude?**

No. The Velo Slack Agent and Velo MCP never delete anything. Delete from **Recent chats**.

**Do pinned chats show for my teammates?**

  No. Pins are personal.
