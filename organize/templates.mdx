## Use a template from the gallery

1. On the home screen, open the template gallery below the chat box.
2. Choose a template from your team, or one of Velo's.
3. Open it to preview the sample video.
4. Select **Use this template**. Its starter prompt fills the chat box.
5. Replace the placeholders in brackets, then select **Send**.

## Use a template by name

Name it in chat, and Velo applies it. If two templates match, Velo asks which one you mean.

```text
Use our Launch template to make a 45-second video about our new Assets page.
```

## Velo's templates and your team's

- **Velo's templates:** Ready-made formats from Velo.
- **Your team's templates:** Made from your workspace's own videos. The preview shows a **Team template** badge and who made it. See [Convert to a template](/organize/convert-to-a-template).
- **Workspace only:** Your team's templates stay inside your workspace.

## Rename or delete a template

The person who made it, or an admin or owner, can manage it.

1. Open the template's card.
2. To rename it, select its name and type a new one.
3. To delete it, select **Delete** and confirm.

Deleting a template removes it from the gallery and chat. Videos already made from it keep working.

## FAQs

**Does a template override my Brand Kit?**

No. Your Brand Kit applies on top of any template.

**Can teammates in other workspaces use our templates?**

No. Templates stay inside the workspace they were made in.

**Who can delete a template?**

The person who made it, or a workspace admin or owner.
