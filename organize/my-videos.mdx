## Video cards

- **Finished videos:** Play, share or open the chat.
- **In progress:** Videos that haven't rendered yet. Select **Open chat** to keep working.

## Manage a video

Open a card's **⋯** menu to:

- **Rename:** Video names can be up to **60** characters.
- **Transfer:** Hand the video to another member of your workspace. Both of you get an email.
- **Add to library:** Put it in one or more libraries. See [Libraries](/organize/libraries).
- **Thumbnail settings:** Choose its cover image. See [Custom thumbnails](/share/custom-thumbnails).
- **Analytics:** See who watched. See [Track views](/share/track-views).

To act on several videos at once, select them, then choose an action.

<Tip>
There's no Delete here. To delete a video, delete its chat in **Recent chats**. See [Recent chats](/organize/recent-chats).
</Tip>

## Transfer rules

- **Who can transfer:** A video's owner can transfer it. Workspace owners and admins can transfer anyone's video.
- **When:** You can't transfer a video while the agent is working on it.

## Inspiration gallery

Before you make your first video, My videos shows six example videos, grouped by team. Open one to see what Velo can make.

## FAQs

**How do I delete a video?**

Delete its chat in **Recent chats** in the sidebar. Deleting the chat deletes the video.

**Why is my video "In progress"?**

It hasn't been rendered yet. Open its chat to finish it, or select **Export**.

**Can I move a video to another workspace?**

No. Transfer moves a video to another member of the same workspace.
