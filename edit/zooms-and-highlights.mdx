## Add an effect

1. Move the playhead to the moment.
2. On the timeline, select **+**.
3. Choose **Zoom**, **Spotlight**, **Mask** or **Callout**.
4. Drag the box on the preview to the area you want.
5. Drag the layer's edges on the timeline to set how long it lasts.

## Effects

- **Zoom:** Moves in on an area, then back out.
- **Spotlight:** Dims everything except one area.
- **Mask:** Covers or blurs an area, such as private data.
- **Callout:** Adds a label that points at something.

<Tip>
To skip the timeline, comment on the moment and ask for a zoom (for example, "Zoom in on Export at 0:12").
</Tip>

## FAQs

**Can Velo add zooms for me?**

Yes. Velo adds zooms when it builds the video, and you can ask for more in chat.

**How do I remove an effect?**

Select its layer on the timeline and press `Delete`.

**Can I hide private details with a mask?**

Yes. For several details, it's faster to ask Velo. See [Hide private details](/edit/hide-private-details).
