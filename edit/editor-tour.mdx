## Layout

Once a video is built, its chat opens in a split view.

- **Chat:** Ask Velo for changes in plain language. See [Edit by chat and comments](/edit/edit-by-chat-and-comments).
- **Preview:** Play the video. Switch to comment mode to point at a spot.
- **Timeline:** Every layer of the video, in order. See [Work with the timeline](/edit/work-with-the-timeline).

## Left rail

- **Agent:** The chat with the Velo Agent.
- **Assets:** Media you can add to the timeline.
- **Audio:** Music, narration volume and voice. See [Music and audio](/edit/music-and-audio).
- **Comments:** Your team's comments on the video.
- **Settings:** Output size, background and video details. See [Canvas settings](/edit/canvas-settings).

## Header actions

- **Version history:** See and restore earlier versions. See [Version history](/edit/version-history).
- **Convert to a template:** Save this video's look for your team. See [Convert to a template](/organize/convert-to-a-template).
- **Export:** Render the video and get a share link. See [Share a link](/share/share-a-link).

## Insert menu

Select **+** on the timeline to add an element: **Text**, **Image**, **Rectangle**, **Arrow**, **Video**, **Audio**, **Zoom**, **Spotlight**, **Mask**, **Callout** or **Comment**. Each layer has its own settings panel. See [Add text, shapes and media](/edit/add-text-shapes-and-media).

## FAQs

**Should I edit by chat or by hand?**

Either. Chat is fastest for most changes. Use the timeline for exact timing.

**Can I undo a change?**

Yes. Press `⌘Z` for editor changes, or restore an earlier version for agent changes.

**Where's the full editor?**

It's built in. There's no separate editor to open.
