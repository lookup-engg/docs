## Change the size or background

1. In the left rail, select **Settings**.
2. Choose an aspect ratio.
3. Choose a background type and set it.
4. Play the preview to check the result.

## What's in Settings

- **Aspect ratio:** The shape of your video, such as wide for a website or tall for mobile.
- **Background:** What shows behind your content.
- **Video info:** Details about the video.

## FAQs

**Will changing the aspect ratio crop my video?**

It can. Check each scene in the preview, and ask Velo to reframe anything that's cut off.

**Can I ask Velo to change these instead?**

Yes. For example, "Make this a square video for LinkedIn".
