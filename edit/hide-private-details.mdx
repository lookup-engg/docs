## Hide details by chat

1. Open the video's chat.
2. Say what to hide and where (for example, "Blur the email address at @0:08").
3. Send it.
4. Play the preview and check every scene where the detail appears.

```text
Blur the email address at 0:08, and replace the customer name "Acme" with "Alex" everywhere it appears.
```

## What Velo can do

- **Blur:** Blur text or an area, and fix blur timing so it covers the whole moment.
- **Replace names:** Swap a real name for a stand-in name.
- **Remove objects:** Take something off the screen.

To point at the exact spot, use a comment. See [Edit by chat and comments](/edit/edit-by-chat-and-comments).

<Warning>
Check the result before you share. A detail can appear in more than one scene.
</Warning>

## FAQs

**Can I blur something by hand?**

Yes. Add a **Mask** from the Insert menu. See [Zooms and highlights](/edit/zooms-and-highlights).

**Does hiding a detail change my original recording?**

No. It changes the video. Restore an earlier version to undo it.

**Can I stop people outside my company from watching?**

Yes. Add a passcode or require an email. See [Secure links](/share/secure-links).
