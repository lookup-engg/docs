Split clips at the playhead and crop the frame to show only what matters.

> *Placeholder (gif): the timeline highlighted, pressing S to split at the playhead, Shift+S to split every clip, then dragging a crop handle on the canvas*

## Split a clip

1. Select the clip on the timeline.
2. Move the playhead to where you want to cut.
3. Press `S`.

To split every clip at the playhead at once, press `Shift+S`.

## Crop the frame

1. Select the layer.
2. On the preview, drag the crop handles to the area you want to keep.

## FAQs

**How do I remove part of a clip?**

Split it on both sides of the part, select that part, and press `Delete`.

**Can I undo a split?**

Yes. Press `⌘Z`. The AI Editor keeps your last **50** steps.

**Can Velo cut the dead time for me?**

Yes. Ask in chat (for example, "Cut the pauses in the first scene").
