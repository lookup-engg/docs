## Add a layer

1. Move the playhead to where the layer should start.
2. On the timeline, select **+**.
3. Choose an element.
4. Adjust it in its settings panel.

## Elements

- **Text:** Titles, labels and on-screen notes.
- **Image:** A picture or logo.
- **Rectangle** and **Arrow:** Shapes that point things out.
- **Video** and **Audio:** Your own clips, voice-over or music.
- **Zoom**, **Spotlight**, **Mask** and **Callout:** Effects. See [Zooms and highlights](/edit/zooms-and-highlights).
- **Comment:** A note for your team or for Velo.

## Use your own media

The AI Editor's **Assets** panel holds media you can add: video, audio and images. Documents can't go on the timeline. See [Supported formats](/help/supported-formats).

## FAQs

**Can I use files from my workspace's Assets?**

Yes, media files. Documents are used as sources when Velo writes a video, not as layers.

**Can I upload my own music?**

Yes. Add an **Audio** layer, or upload it in the **Audio** panel. See [Music and audio](/edit/music-and-audio).

**Can Velo add the text for me?**

Yes. Ask in chat (for example, "Add a title card that says Getting started").
