Set the music, volume and narration voice for your video.

> *Placeholder (gif): the Audio panel in the left rail highlighted, picking a music track, lowering the volume, uploading an audio file, then playing the preview*

## Change music and volume

1. In the left rail, select **Audio**.
2. Pick a track from the music list, or upload your own.
3. Adjust the music and narration volume.
4. Play the preview to check the mix.

## Change the voice

Use the **Change voice** quick action, or ask in chat (for example, "Narrate with my voice Vanessa"). Velo re-voices the narration. See [Quick actions](/edit/quick-actions).

## Fix how a word sounds

Add the word to **Brand Kit** → **Pronunciations**, so every new video says it right. See [Brand Kit](/organize/brand-kit#set-up-pronunciation). To fix it in one video only, ask in chat.

## FAQs

**Can I remove the music?**

Yes. Remove the track in the **Audio** panel, or ask Velo for no music.

**Can I use my own music?**

Yes. Upload it in the **Audio** panel. See [Supported formats](/help/supported-formats).

**Does changing the voice use credits?**

Yes. Velo re-voices the narration, which is an agent run.
