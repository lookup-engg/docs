## Use a quick action

1. Open the video's chat.
2. Select a quick action under the chat, such as **Change voice**.
3. Make your choice (for example, pick a new voice).
4. Play the updated preview.

## Common quick actions

The actions shown depend on the video. They include:

- **Change voice:** Pick a different narrator.
- **Add avatar:** Add a presenter. See [Generate with Avatar](/create/generate-with-avatar).
- **Add BGM:** Add background music.
- **Change script:** Rewrite the narration.
- **Render Video:** Render the latest version.

## FAQs

**Why don't I see the same quick actions on every video?**

They vary with the kind of video. For anything not listed, ask in chat.

**Do quick actions use credits?**

Yes. Each one is an agent run, like a chat edit.

**Can I undo a quick action?**

Yes. Restore an earlier version. See [Version history](/edit/version-history).
