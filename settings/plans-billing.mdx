Plans and billing are managed at the workspace level. Navigate to **Settings → Plans & Billing** to view your current plan and manage billing.

<Note>
  This page covers classic Velo minutes. Prompt to Video draws from a separate credit pool — see [Prompt to Video billing & credits](/prompt-to-video/billing-credits).
</Note>

## Plans tab

The **Plans** tab shows your current plan and the upgrade options available. Each plan includes a set number of Velo minutes, seats, and features. See the [Pricing](/pricing/pricing) page for a full comparison.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings/plans-billing/01.png" alt="Plans & Billing page showing plan options" />
</Frame>

Click **Upgrade to Pro** or **Upgrade to Ultra** to switch plans.

## Top-up tab

If you run out before your cycle resets, you can buy more from the **Top-up** tab. It has two sub-tabs — **Velo minutes** and **Reach credits** — because the two pools are metered separately.

### Velo minutes

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings/plans-billing/02.png" alt="Top-up tab showing the Velo minutes pack" />
</Frame>

- **15 minutes** for **$5.99** ($0.40/min)

### Reach credits

Credits for [Prompt to Video](/prompt-to-video/overview), sold in four packs:

| Credits | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 400 | $10.00 |
| 1,000 | $25.00 |
| 4,000 | $100.00 |
| 10,000 | $250.00 |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings/plans-billing/03.png" alt="Top-up tab showing the Reach credit packs" />
</Frame>

An **Order Summary** shows the package, unit price, and total before you confirm. Click **Top up account** to complete the purchase — the balance is added to your workspace pool immediately and does not expire at the end of the cycle.

## Invoices tab

View and download past invoices for your workspace.

## Payment methods tab

Add, update, or remove payment methods used for plan subscriptions and top-ups.
