Account security settings live at the bottom of **Settings → Profile**, below your profile details.

## Reset password

Select **Reset Password** to start a password reset. Velo sends a reset link to your registered email address.

## Devices

The **Devices** table lists every session currently signed in to your account:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Device** | Browser and operating system. Your current session is tagged **This device** |
| **IP address** | The address the session connected from |
| **Last active** | When that session last used Velo |

Select **Log out** on any row to end that one session — useful if you recognise a device you no longer use.

**Log out of all devices** ends every session, including the one you are currently using, so you will be signed out immediately and need to sign in again. Reach for it if you think someone else has access to your account.

<Note>
  Signing out of a device does not affect the workspace or any videos in it. It only ends that browser session.
</Note>

## Deleting your account

The **Danger Zone** at the bottom of the page holds **Delete account**, which permanently deletes your account and removes your access from every workspace.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you are the only owner of a workspace, transfer ownership to another member first — see [Roles and permissions](/workspaces/roles-permissions).
</Warning>
