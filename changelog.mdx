<Update label="Nov 24, 2026" rss={{ title:"Brand Kit pronunciations and Intro/Outro",description:"You can now add a word or phrase on the Pronunciations tab of your Brand Kit, describe how it should sound, select Generate voice to hear it, and save it." }}>
  ### Brand Kit pronunciations and Intro/Outro

  Teach Velo how to say your names, and use your own intro and outro

  No more product names that sound wrong in narration.

  You can now add a word or phrase on the Pronunciations tab of your Brand Kit, describe how it should sound, select Generate voice to hear it, and save it, so every new video in your workspace says it your way instead of you having to respell it in each script.

  A few things worth knowing:

  - Subtitles and on-screen text keep the spelling as written, and every voice says the term the same way.
  - Workspace admins and owners add and edit terms. Everyone else can see and play them.
  - Prefer your own branding clips? In Brand Kit > Intro/Outro, select Upload your own to use your intro, outro, or both (H.264 MP4 or MOV).

  Learn more in [Set up pronunciation](/organize/brand-kit#set-up-pronunciation) and [Intro/Outro](/organize/brand-kit#intro-outro).
</Update>

<Update label="Nov 23, 2026" rss={{ title:"Replace and Regenerate",description:"You can now replace a file in your Asset Library and then regenerate the videos that still use the old one." }}>
  ### Replace and Regenerate

  Change a file once, update every video that uses it

  No more remaking videos when a logo or screenshot changes.

  You can now replace a file in your Asset Library and then regenerate the videos that still use the old one, so every video picks up the new file instead of you having to find and fix each video yourself.

  A few things worth knowing:

  - After you replace a file, videos that still use the old version are marked so you can review them.
  - Pick the videos to update and select Regenerate, and Velo updates each one in its own chat.
  - Only your own videos can be regenerated, and updates use your credits.

  Learn more in [Asset Library](/organize/assets#replace-a-file-and-update-your-videos).
</Update>

  ### Velo Slack Agent

  Make videos without leaving Slack

  No more leaving the conversation to start a video.

  You can now create videos by tagging @Velo in any channel or group, or by messaging it directly, so a video starts right where the discussion is happening instead of you having to switch to the web app.

  A few things worth knowing:

  - Connect your account to your Velo workspace first.
  - Videos you make in Slack land in that workspace with everything else.

  Learn more in [Velo Slack Agent](/connect/slack).
</Update>

<Update label="Nov 13, 2026" rss={{ title:"Templates",description:"You can now select Convert to a template on a video you made, review what Velo picked out, and save it for your workspace." }}>
  ### Templates

  Turn your own videos into templates for your team

  No more rebuilding a format each time someone needs the same kind of video.

  You can now select Convert to a template on a video you made, review what Velo picked out, and save it for your workspace, so teammates start from your design, voice, and structure instead of you having to explain them again.

  A few things worth knowing:

  - Find Convert to a template beside Export in the editor, or in the "..." menu on a video card.
  - You choose what the template keeps, like fonts, colors, narration voice, face clone, and Intro/Outro, and pick a category.
  - Teammates find your templates under Custom in "Start with a template".

  Learn more in [Convert to a template](/organize/convert-to-a-template).
</Update>

<Update label="Nov 11, 2026" rss={{ title:"Velo MCP",description:"You can now ask for a video from your AI assistant with Velo for ChatGPT and Velo for Claude." }}>
  ### Velo MCP

  Make videos from ChatGPT and Claude

  No more switching apps to turn an idea into a video.

  You can now ask for a video from your AI assistant with Velo for ChatGPT and Velo for Claude, so Velo makes it in your workspace and sends back the link instead of you having to open Velo and start over.

  A few things worth knowing:

  - In ChatGPT, open Plugins in the left sidebar, search for Velo, and sign in.
  - In Claude, copy the connection URL from Connectors > Velo MCP in Velo, and add it as a custom connector.
  - Start your request with "Use Velo to…" so your assistant knows to hand it over.

  Learn more in [Velo MCP](/connect/velo-mcp).
</Update>

<Update label="Nov 6, 2026" rss={{ title:"Editor comments",description:"You can now switch the editor to Comment, select the spot on the video you want changed, and type what you want." }}>
  ### Editor comments

  Comment on the exact frame you want changed

  No more describing where in the video a change should go.

  You can now switch the editor to Comment, select the spot on the video you want changed, and type what you want, so Velo gets the exact frame and the element you picked instead of you having to explain it in chat.

  A few things worth knowing:

  - You can attach files to a comment, like a new screenshot or logo.
  - Finished comments move to the Comments tab, so you can still read them later.
  - Prefer chat? Ask for changes in the chat the same way as before.

  Learn more in [Edit by chat and comments](/edit/edit-by-chat-and-comments#comments).
</Update>

<Update label="Sep 29, 2026" rss={{ title:"Velo Agent",description:"You can now tell the Velo Agent what you need in one chat, and it picks the right path for a screen recording, a document, a link, or a prompt." }}>
  ### Velo Agent

  One agent for every video you make

  No more choosing a different flow for each kind of video.

  You can now tell the Velo Agent what you need in one chat, and it picks the right path for a screen recording, a document, a link, or a prompt, so you ask for a video the same way every time instead of you having to know which tool to open.

  A few things worth knowing:

  - Record Yourself, Agentic Recording, and Avatars now start from the same chat.
  - Set up your Brand Kit once, and every new video uses your logo, colors, and fonts.
  - Prefer to edit by hand? The AI Editor gives you a full timeline that also takes plain-language instructions.

  Learn more in [How the Velo Agent works](/get-started/how-the-velo-agent-works).
</Update>

  ### Prompt to video

  Start a video from a sentence

  No recording, file, or link needed to get going.

  - Add a Knowledge Base, like your website or docs, so the script matches your actual product.

  Until today, Velo was built for you. Starting now, it's built for your whole team.

  - Shared voices & faces - Your best voice clones and face clones, available to the whole team for consistent output.

  _You can now tweak your script straight from the preview pane and generate the voiceover on the spot._

  Make a change, hit generate, and hear it read back in seconds.

  A few things that make this smoother:

  - When you ask for a rewrite, you can now tell it exactly what you want, like: shorten this part, make it punchier, change the tone - instead of accepting whatever comes back and editing by hand.
  - The character limit that used to cap how much you could change is gone, so you're free to rework a line or rewrite the whole thing without bumping into a wall.
  - And if your script is based on a PDF or an uploaded file, you can now re-generate a script and voiceover from it just like any other flow - no need to retype or paste it in somewhere else first.
