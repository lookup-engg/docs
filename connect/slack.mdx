## How it works

- **The thread is the brief:** Velo reads the conversation, so you don't retype the request.
- **Questions come to Slack:** Velo asks its questions as cards, with choices, voice and avatar pickers, and a box for your own answer.
- **The video comes back to the thread:** Velo posts the finished video file with a link to open it in Velo.
- **Everyone can follow along:** Anyone in the thread can ask for a change, and each change is saved as a version.

## Connect Slack to your workspace

An admin or owner connects Slack once for the whole workspace.

1. In the sidebar, open **Connectors**, then **Slack**.
2. Select **Add to Slack**.
3. Approve Velo in Slack.

What happens next: Velo matches your teammates to their Velo accounts by their Slack email. One Slack connection links to one Velo workspace.

## Make a video from a thread

1. In a channel, thread, group chat or direct message, tag **@Velo** and describe the video.
2. Answer Velo's questions in the thread.
3. Open the video file or the Velo link that Velo posts back.
4. To change it, reply in the thread (for example, "make it shorter").

```text
@Velo Turn this thread into a 45-second walkthrough for new customers.
```

While Velo works, Slack shows that it's working, with a **Stop** button. Stopping keeps what Velo has made so far.

## Ask about your Velo

You can ask @Velo about your workspace without opening the web app.

- **Find things:** List your videos, voices, avatars, templates, libraries and Brand Kit.
- **Manage a video:** Rename it, restore an earlier version, or get a share or download link.
- **Transfer a video:** Hand a video to another member of your workspace. Both of you get an email.

## What opens the web app

Velo never deletes anything from Slack. For these, it points you to the Velo web app:

- Deleting videos, templates, voices, avatars or libraries
- Creating voice or face clones
- Knowledge bases
- Credits and usage
- Members and invites
- Background music choices

## Who owns the video and whose credits it uses

- **Owner:** The person who first tags Velo in a thread owns its video. They can open and edit it in the web app.
- **Credits:** Requests use the credits of the sender's workspace, the same as the web app. Until a video exists, usage shows as **Velo in Slack** in your usage.
- **Teammates without a Velo account:** Velo tells them how to join, and an admin gets an **Invite** button.
- **People from other companies:** In a shared channel, they select **Connect my Velo account**. Their videos are made in their own workspace, with their own credits.

## FAQs

**Can I use @Velo in a direct message?**

Yes. @Velo works in channels, threads, group chats and direct messages.

**Why did Velo ask me to try again in a minute?**

Slack limits how fast Velo can read threads, because Velo isn't listed in the Slack Marketplace yet. Wait a minute and tag @Velo again.

**Can I keep editing a Slack video in Velo?**

Yes. Open the Velo link in the thread. The video and all its versions are in your workspace.

**Velo doesn't recognize me. What do I do?**

Select **Connect my Velo account** and sign in. Velo matches people by their Slack email, so this happens when your emails differ.
