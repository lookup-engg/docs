Connectors give Velo your product context, or let you use Velo from other tools.

## Connectors that give Velo context

| Connector | What it does | Who can add it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Knowledge Bases](/connect/knowledge-bases) | Velo crawls your website or docs and uses them when it writes scripts | Anyone. Promote one to share it with your workspace |
| [Applications](/connect/applications) | Velo pulls context from tools your team uses, such as Notion or HubSpot | Anyone, with their own account for the tool |
| [MCP servers](/connect/mcp-servers) | Velo reads from your own MCP server, for internal tools | Anyone  |

## Connectors that bring Velo to other tools

| Connector | What it does | Who can add it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Velo MCP](/connect/velo-mcp) | Make and manage Velo videos from Claude or ChatGPT | Anyone, for their own AI tool |
| [Velo Slack Agent](/connect/slack) | Tag @Velo in Slack to make videos from a thread | Admins and owners, once per workspace |

All connectors live under **Connectors** in the sidebar.

## FAQs

**Do I need a connector to make a video?**

No. Velo works from your request alone. Connectors add context, so scripts match your real product.

**What's the difference between Velo MCP and MCP servers?**

Velo MCP lets you use Velo inside Claude or ChatGPT. MCP servers let Velo read from your own tools.

**Are files a connector?**

No. Upload files to [Asset Library](/organize/assets) instead.
