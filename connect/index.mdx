## FAQs

**Who can connect Slack?**

Admins and owners, once for the whole workspace.

**Can Velo delete videos from Slack or Claude?**

No. The Velo Slack Agent and Velo MCP never delete anything.

**Do I need a connector to make a video?**

No. Connectors add context, but Velo works from your request alone.
