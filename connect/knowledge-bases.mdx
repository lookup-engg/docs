## Add a knowledge base

1. In the sidebar, select **Connectors**, then **Knowledge bases**.
2. Select **Add knowledge base**.
3. Enter a **Name** and the **Seed URL**, the page Velo starts from.
4. Choose whether to download images and media.
5. Select **Continue**. Velo maps the site.
6. In **Select pages**, pick the pages to crawl. Search to find them.
7. Confirm.

What happens next: when the crawl finishes, the knowledge base shows as **Connected**. It lists pages crawled, media files and the last crawl date.

<Tip>
Pick only the pages that matter for your videos. Fewer, focused pages give Velo clearer context.
</Tip>

## Use it in a video

Ask for it in your request (for example, "Use our Help Center knowledge base"). Velo uses it when it writes the script.

## Keep it current

- **Re-ingest:** Crawl the site again to pick up changes.
- **Promote to workspace:** Share the knowledge base with everyone in your workspace.

## Limits

- **Pages per crawl:** **50** by default, up to **10,000**.
- **Select pages:** Shows the first **1,000** pages. Use search to find others.

## FAQs

**Does Velo update the knowledge base when my site changes?**

No. Select **Re-ingest** to crawl it again.

**Does crawling a site use credits?**

No. Crawling and re-ingesting are free. Credits are used only when you make a video that uses the knowledge base.

**Can my teammates use my knowledge base?**

Yes, once you select **Promote to workspace**.

**Should I use a knowledge base or Assets?**

Use a knowledge base for websites and docs. Use [Asset Library](/organize/assets) for files such as decks and screenshots.
