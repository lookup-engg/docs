## How it works

- **Velo runs inside your AI tool:** Velo MCP adds Velo's tools to Claude or ChatGPT. You ask in plain language, and Velo does the work in your workspace.
- **Videos land in your workspace:** Every video you make this way shows up in Velo, ready to edit, share and track.
- **The answer comes back to the chat:** When the video is ready, you get a link to it in the same conversation.

## Before you start

- A Velo account and a workspace to make videos in.
- Claude or ChatGPT, signed in.

## Velo for ChatGPT

1. In ChatGPT, open **Plugins** in the left sidebar.
2. Search for **Velo**.
3. Select Velo and connect it.
4. Sign in to Velo when asked, and choose the workspace to use.

## Velo for Claude

1. In Velo, open **Connectors** in the sidebar, then **Velo MCP**.
2. Copy the connection URL.
3. In Claude, add Velo as a custom connector and paste the URL.
4. Sign in to Velo when asked.

What happens next: Velo appears as a connected tool in your AI chat. In Velo, **Connectors** → **Velo MCP** lists each connected AI tool.

## Make a video from your AI chat

1. Start a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT.
2. Ask for the video, and mention Velo (for example, "Use Velo to…").
3. Answer any questions Velo asks.
4. Open the link Velo sends back to watch the video.

```text
Use Velo to make a 45-second walkthrough of our new Assets page for new customers. Use our Brand Kit and end on our website.
```

## What Velo MCP can do

- **Make and edit videos:** Start a video, ask for changes, stop a run, export and rename.
- **Versions:** List a video's versions and restore an earlier one.
- **Share:** Get a share or download link, change share settings and check a video's stats.
- **Your workspace:** Work with libraries, templates, the Brand Kit and shared clones, and transfer a video to a teammate.
- **What it can't do:** Delete anything. To delete a video, use **Recent chats** in the Velo web app.

## Connection and limits

- **Staying signed in:** A connection stays signed in for **30** days from the last time you use it. After that, reconnect it.
- **Upload links:** Links Velo gives you for uploading files expire after **30** minutes.
- **Request limits:** Each tool accepts a limited number of requests per minute. If you hit the limit, wait a minute and try again.
- **Disconnect:** In **Connectors** → **Velo MCP**, select **Disconnect** next to the AI tool.

## FAQs

**Do videos made from Claude or ChatGPT use my credits?**

Yes. They use your workspace's credit balance, the same as videos made in the Velo web app.

**Can I use one of my workspace's templates?**

Yes. Name the template in your request, and Velo builds the video from it.

**Why did Velo stop working in Claude?**

The connection expires **30** days after you last used it. Reconnect Velo, and your videos are still in your workspace.

**Can I delete a video from my AI chat?**

No. Velo MCP never deletes anything. Delete the video's chat from **Recent chats** in the web app.
