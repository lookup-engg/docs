## Make a video from a link

1. On the home screen, select **Turn URL to video**.
2. Paste the link and describe the flow to show.
3. Answer the agent's questions.
4. Watch Agentic Recording work in the live view in the chat.
5. Approve the script.

```text
Go to usevelo.ai, open the pricing page and show how to compare plans. Make it 45 seconds, for new visitors.
```

## Take control

When a page needs you, such as a sign-in, take over the browser.

1. Select **Take control**.
2. Sign in or finish the step.
3. Hand control back to the agent.

You can take control for up to **10** minutes.

## Cancel

Select **Cancel recording** to stop Agentic Recording.

## FAQs

**Does Agentic Recording use credits the whole time?**

Yes. It uses credits while it explores and records, so keep requests focused on one flow.

**Will it record private data?**

It records whatever it sees. Use a demo account, or ask Velo to blur details afterward. See [Hide private details](/edit/hide-private-details).

**What if the agent gets stuck?**

Take control to move it past the step, or cancel and describe the flow more precisely.
