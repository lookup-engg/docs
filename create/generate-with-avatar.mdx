## Make an avatar video

1. On the home screen, select **Generate with Avatar**.
2. Paste your script, or ask Velo to write one.
3. Pick a face: a public avatar, your own face clone, or one shared with your workspace.
4. Pick a voice.
5. Choose where the avatar sits, such as full frame or in a corner over your product.
6. Answer any other questions, then approve the script.

Velo generates the avatar and builds the video. Velo emails you when your avatar is ready.

```text
Add my avatar Vanessa in the bottom right corner, narrating with my voice. Full frame for the intro and outro.
```

## Faces, voices and placement

- **Public avatars:** Ready-made faces and voices anyone can use. See [Public voices and avatars](/voices-and-avatars/public-voices-and-avatars).
- **Your face and voice:** Use your own clones. See [Clone your face](/voices-and-avatars/clone-your-face) and [Clone your voice](/voices-and-avatars/clone-your-voice).
- **Placement:** Put the avatar full frame, or over a screen demo, in the position you choose.
- **Intro and outro:** By default, the avatar opens and closes the video. Ask Velo if you want it elsewhere.

## Credits and limits

- **Billed by length:** Avatar video uses credits per generated second, with a **5**-second minimum per clip.
- **Free plan:** One avatar face per billing cycle. After that, you'll see **You've used your avatar face for this billing cycle. Upgrade for more.**

## FAQs

**Can I make the avatar look like me?**

Yes. Create a face clone, then pick it in the face list or name it in your prompt.

**Can I add an avatar to a video I already made?**

Yes. Use the **Add avatar** quick action, or ask in chat. See [Quick actions](/edit/quick-actions).

**Why does my avatar appear at the start and end?**

The agent adds an avatar intro and outro by default. Ask Velo to remove them or move the avatar.
