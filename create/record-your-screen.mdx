## Before you start

- Chrome with the [Velo Companion extension](/create/velo-companion-extension) installed and signed in.
- The workspace you'll edit the video in. Recordings stay in the workspace you record in.

## Record your screen

1. On the home screen, select **Record Screen**.
2. In the Companion panel, check the **workspace**.
3. Choose your microphone and the output voice.
5. Select **Start Recording**.
6. Choose a Chrome tab, a window or your entire screen, then select **Share**.
7. Do the task you want to show, explaining it as you go.
8. Select **Stop** to finish.

Velo uploads the recording and builds a narrated draft. Longer recordings can take a few minutes to process. That's normal.

## Fix common messages

- **Install the Velo extension to record your screen.** Install or reinstall the [Velo Companion extension](/create/velo-companion-extension), then try again.
- **That recording is taking too long to process.** Try again. If it keeps happening, record a shorter take.
- **We couldn't find that recording in this workspace.** Switch to the workspace you recorded in, or record again.

## FAQs

**Can I record sound from a tab?**

Yes. In the share dialog, turn on **Also share tab audio** before you select **Share**.

**Why can't I find my recording?**

You're probably in a different workspace. Switch to the one you recorded in.

**Will private details on screen show in the video?**

Yes, unless you hide them. Ask Velo to blur them. See [Hide private details](/edit/hide-private-details).
