## FAQs

**What files can I start from?**

Video, audio, images, PDFs, Office files and text, up to **10** per message. See [Supported formats](/help/supported-formats).

**Can I pick which PDF pages Velo uses?**

Yes. Name the pages in your request. There's no page picker.

**Does Velo make anything before I approve?**

No. Velo waits for you to approve the script.
