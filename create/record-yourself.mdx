## How it works

- **You stay you:** Velo keeps your real face, voice and delivery.
- **Velo adds the product:** It switches between you, full-screen product footage and both together in picture-in-picture.
- **One take is enough:** Velo cuts mistakes, repeated sentences and long silences without changing your meaning.

## Before you start

- A camera and microphone, and permission for your browser to use them.
- Product footage or screenshots, if you have them. Attach them, or keep them in [Asset Library](/organize/assets).

## Record your take

1. On the home screen, select **Record Yourself**.
2. Choose **Record on Velo**. To use a video you already filmed, choose **Upload video**.
3. Write your script, paste one, or ask Velo to write it.
4. Set the teleprompter size and speed.
5. Record your take, reading from the teleprompter.
6. Review the plan Velo shows you, then approve it.

Velo builds the video from your take and your product context. This can take a few minutes.

<Tip>
Place the teleprompter window near your camera, so you look at the viewer while you read.
</Tip>

## FAQs

**Do I need to get every line right?**

No. Velo cuts mistakes, repeats and pauses.

**Can I add my own product footage?**

Yes. Attach it to the chat, or upload it to Assets first.

**Can I download the finished video?**

Yes. See [Download an MP4](/share/download-an-mp4).
