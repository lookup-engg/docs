Write requests that get the video right the first time, with fewer rounds of edits.

## What a good request includes

- **What done looks like:** What the viewer should know or do when the video ends.
- **Who it's for:** The audience, such as new customers, admins or your sales team.
- **A real source:** A file, link, knowledge base or asset. Velo works from facts instead of guessing.
- **The steps in order:** For a how-to, list the on-screen actions in the order they happen.
- **What to stress and skip:** The one point that matters most, and anything to leave out.
- **Length and tone:** For example, "45 seconds, calm and direct".

```text
Make a 45-second how-to for new admins on inviting a teammate. Use the attached help article. Show Settings, then Members, then Invite. Stress that invites expire after 7 days. Skip billing.
```

## Give Velo context

- **Attach files:** Up to **10** per message. See [Choose how to start](/create/choose-how-to-start).

- **Connect sources:** Knowledge bases and connected apps add product context. See [Connectors overview](/connect/connectors-overview).

## Match a reference video

Attach a video whose style you like, and use one of these prompts.

To make a new video in that style:

```text
Use the attached video as the style reference. Match its look and feel: background, colors, typography, motion graphics, effects, transitions and pacing. Make a [45-second] [product showreel] about [feature] for [audience]. Use our logo, our brand colors and the attached [script / product facts]. Don't copy the reference's words, logo or product. End on [logo and website].
```

To restyle a video you already made:

```text
Restyle this video to match the attached reference. Keep my script, scenes, voiceover and length. Change the look to match the reference: background, colors, typography, motion, effects and transitions. Don't copy the reference's words or logo.
```

## FAQs

**Should I use Enhance prompt?**

Yes, for a short request. Read the result before sending, because it can add things you didn't ask for.

**How long should my request be?**

As long as it needs to cover the points above. A few clear sentences usually beat one long paragraph.

**Why did Velo ask me so many questions?**

Your request left things open. Add the audience, length and source next time, or select **Decide all for me**.
