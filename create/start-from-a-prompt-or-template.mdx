## Start from a prompt

1. On the home screen, type your request in the chat box.
2. Say who it's for, what they should be able to do afterward, and how long it should be.
3. Select **Send**.
4. Answer the agent's questions, then approve the script.

```text
Make a 45-second video for new admins on inviting teammates. Show where Members is, and end on our website.
```

## Start from a template

1. Below the chat box, open the template gallery.
2. Choose a Velo template, or one from your team.
3. Select **Use this template**. The template's starter prompt fills the chat box.
4. Replace the placeholders in brackets, such as [PRODUCT].
5. Select **Send**.

<Note>
If you remove a template, delete any leftover placeholders in the chat box before you send.
</Note>

You can also name a template in chat (for example, "Use our Launch template").

## What a good request includes

- **The outcome:** What the viewer should know or do afterward.
- **The audience:** Who's watching, such as new customers or your sales team.
- **Real sources:** A file, link or knowledge base, so Velo doesn't guess.
- **What to stress and skip:** The one point that matters most, and anything to leave out.

## FAQs

**What's the difference between Velo's templates and my team's?**

Velo's templates are ready-made formats. Your team's templates are made from your own videos and keep your workspace's look.

**Does a template replace my Brand Kit?**

No. Your Brand Kit applies on top of any template.

**Can I change the video after it's built?**

Yes. Ask for changes in the chat. See [Edit by chat and comments](/edit/edit-by-chat-and-comments).
