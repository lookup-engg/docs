## Before you start

- An MP4 or MOV file, up to **30** minutes and **500 MB**.

## Polish your recording

1. On the home screen, select **Polish a recording**.
2. Upload your file.
3. Tell Velo what the video is for and what to cut or stress.
4. Answer the agent's questions.
5. Approve the script.

Velo watches and transcribes your recording first, then asks its questions. Longer recordings take a few minutes.

```text
Cut the pauses and the part where I log in. Zoom in on each button I use, and narrate it for new customers.
```

## What Velo does

- **Cleanup:** Cuts pauses, dead time and mistakes.
- **Zooms and highlights:** Draws attention to what you select.
- **Narration:** Writes and voices a script, in a public voice or your voice clone.

## FAQs

**My recording is longer than 30 minutes. What do I do?**

Trim it or split it into parts under **30** minutes, then upload each part.

**Can Velo hide private details in my recording?**

Yes. Ask it to blur emails or replace names. See [Hide private details](/edit/hide-private-details).

**Can I keep my own voice from the recording?**

Ask Velo in your request. You can also narrate with your voice clone.
