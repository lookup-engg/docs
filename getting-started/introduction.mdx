Welcome to the Velo documentation.

Start from a screen recording, a document, a link, or a prompt. Velo reasons through the source material, writes the script, renders the video, and gives you one link to share and track.

With Velo for Teams, your entire team works in a shared [workspace](/workspaces/overview). Videos, libraries, brand settings, and minutes are all managed together so everyone stays on the same page.

## Choose how to create your video

There are five ways to start. Pick the one that fits your source material:

  <Card title="Prompt to Video" icon="sparkles" href="/prompt-to-video/overview">

  <Card title="Capture Screen Recording" icon="video" href="/creating-a-velo/capture-screen">

  <Card title="Upload a Recording" icon="arrow-up-from-bracket" href="/creating-a-velo/upload-recording">
