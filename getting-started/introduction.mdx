Welcome to the Velo documentation. Velo is the video layer for work — AI video infrastructure that helps people and companies explain, train, and sell faster.

Start from a screen recording, a document, a link, or a prompt. Velo reasons through the source material, writes the script, renders the video, and gives you one link to share and track.

With Velo for Teams, your entire team works in a shared [workspace](/workspaces/overview). Videos, libraries, brand settings, and minutes are all managed together so everyone stays on the same page.
