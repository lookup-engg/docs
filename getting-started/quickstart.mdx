Velo takes a raw screen recording or document and renders a narrated video from it — cleaning the audio, pacing the delivery, and generating narration grounded in the source. This guide covers everything from sign-up to your first shared video.

## Create your account and workspace

**Step 1:** Sign up at [app.usevelo.ai](https://app.usevelo.ai) using your email or Google account.

**Step 2:** After signing in, you'll be prompted to set up your first workspace. Give it a name (you can rename it later) and optionally upload a logo.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workspaces/create-workspace/01.png" alt="Set up your workspace dialog" />
</Frame>

Your workspace is where all your team's videos, libraries, and brand settings live. You can [invite teammates](/workspaces/invite-members) later from the workspace settings.

## Choose where to start

The home screen has three tabs, and each one opens a different way in:

- **Start from scratch** — two cards. *Start with a screen recording* captures your screen live through the Velo extension. *Start with a prompt* builds a video from a description, with no source material at all.
- **Upload Video/PDF** — drop in an MP4, MOV or PDF you already have.
- **Paste a link** — hand Velo a URL and its browser agent walks the site for you.

Below the tabs, **Shortcuts** jump straight into common jobs — *Polish your recording*, *PDF to Animated video*, *PDF to Narrated video*, *Animated explainer video* — and a gallery shows finished examples by category.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/creating-a-velo/overview/01.png" alt="The Velo home screen with its three starting tabs, shortcuts and example gallery" />
</Frame>

There is no single right path — pick whichever matches what you already have.

## Upload Content and Choose a Voice

After choosing to upload a recording, select the desired file from your local storage. Once the upload is confirmed, pick a voice. The picker has three tabs — **My Voices** (your own clones), **Organisation Voices** (ones a teammate has promoted for the workspace), and **Public Voices**, a ready-made set described by tone: **Drew** (casual, curious and fun), **Charon** (informative and clear), **Kore** (firm and confident), **Puck** (upbeat and friendly), **Leda** (youthful and bright), **Orus** (firm and steady), and **Aoede** (breezy and natural).

<Frame>
  ![Scene 8](/images/getting-started/quickstart/01.png)
</Frame>

Select a voice profile to handle the automated narration of your video.

## Process and Refine Your Video

Velo drafts a script first, broken into scene cards you can edit, then builds the video once you select **Go, Make my Velo**. A progress card tracks the run — creating the voiceover, then matching the new audio to the video — and the preview appears in the right-hand panel when it's done.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 05 26 At 7 06 37 PM](/images/getting-started/quickstart/02.png)
</Frame>

Velo provides real-time status updates as it processes the video and audio.

Once the video is generated, the **Quick actions** row offers **Disable Zooms**, **Add Music**, **Add Logo**, **Add Face**, **AI Rewrite**, **Publish video** and **Open full editor**. When you're ready to send it out, select **Get a sharable link**.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 05 26 At 7 07 27 PM](/images/getting-started/quickstart/03.png)
</Frame>

Review the final output and use quick actions for last-minute refinements.

The finished video is a narrated video with clean audio and consistent pacing, ready to share.
