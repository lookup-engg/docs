## Make the video

1. On the home screen, type what you want in the chat box. Include the topic, the audience and the length.
2. Select **Send**.
3. Answer the agent's questions, or select **Decide all for me**.
4. Read the script, then select **Approve script**.

Velo builds the video. This can take a few minutes. That's normal, and you can leave the page while Velo works.

```text
Make a 45-second video showing new customers how to create their first project. Friendly tone, end on our website.
```

## Check and edit it

1. Play the preview.
2. Ask for changes in the chat (for example, "Make the intro shorter").

See [Edit by chat and comments](/edit/edit-by-chat-and-comments).

## Share it

1. Select **Export**.
2. Select **Get a sharable link**. Velo renders the video.
3. Copy the link and send it.

What happens next: anyone with the link can watch. You can add a passcode or require an email, and see who watched. See [Share a link](/share/share-a-link).

## FAQs

**How long does a video take?**

A few minutes for most videos. Longer videos and avatar videos take more time.

**Will Velo email me?**

Yes. Velo emails you when your video is ready, when it has rendered, and when your avatar is ready.

**Can I change the video after I share it?**

Yes. Edit it in the chat.
