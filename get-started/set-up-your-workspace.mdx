## The Get Started checklist

The checklist sits on the home screen. Each item opens its own setup page.

| Item | What it does | Who can do it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Create brand kit** | Velo reads your website and applies your logo, fonts and colors to every video | Admins and owners |
| **Create voice clone** | Velo narrates in your voice | Anyone, on Pro or Ultra |
| **Create face avatar** | Velo presents videos with your face | Anyone, on Pro or Ultra |
| **Connect knowledge base** | Velo uses your docs or website for accurate scripts | Anyone |

Members who can't create the Brand Kit see **An admin or owner needs to set this up**.

## Item status

- **Not started:** You haven't opened it yet.
- **In progress:** Setup has started.
- **Needs attention:** Something needs a fix before it's ready.
- **Completed:** Ready to use.

## Work through the checklist

1. On the home screen, find **Get Started**.
2. Select an item.
3. Follow its steps. Each item links to its own page:
   - [Brand Kit](/organize/brand-kit)
   - [Clone your voice](/voices-and-avatars/clone-your-voice)
   - [Clone your face](/voices-and-avatars/clone-your-face)
   - [Knowledge Bases](/connect/knowledge-bases)

## FAQs

**Do I have to finish the checklist before making a video?**

No. You can make videos right away. The checklist makes them match your brand and product.

**Why can't I create the Brand Kit?**

Only admins and owners can. Ask one of them, or see [Workspace, members and roles](/plans-and-account/workspace-members-and-roles).

**Does the checklist use credits?**

Yes, for voice and face clones. Creating a Brand Kit, adding files and crawling a knowledge base don't.
