Velo turns your requests, recordings, documents and links into finished, narrated videos you can share.

## What Velo makes

- **How-to videos and walkthroughs:** Show a product flow step by step.
- **Demos and explainers:** Present a feature with narration, zooms and on-screen text.
- **Presenter videos:** Put your face and voice, or a public avatar, in front of the product.
- **Team videos at scale:** Reuse your Brand Kit, templates and files so every video matches.

## Six ways to start

On the home screen, type a request in the chat box, or pick one of these:

| Option | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| **Record Yourself** | You on camera, reading from a teleprompter |
| **Generate with Avatar** | A script, presented by a public or cloned face |
| **Record Screen** | A recording of your screen, made with the Velo Companion extension |
| **Turn PDF to video** | A PDF, PowerPoint or Word file |
| **Polish a recording** | A video file you already have |
| **Turn URL to video** | A link that Velo's Agentic Recording explores and records |

You can also start from a template. See [Choose how to start](/create/choose-how-to-start).

## How Velo works

1. You tell Velo what you need.
2. The Velo Agent asks a few questions and shows you the script.
3. You approve it, and Velo builds the video.
4. You edit by chat, then share a link.

## Plans at a glance

- **Free:** Try Velo with public voices and avatars.
- **Pro:** For one person making videos every week, with voice and face clones and a Brand Kit.
- **Ultra:** For teams, with more credits and seats.
- **Enterprise:** A custom plan for companies. [Book a demo](https://usevelo.ai).

Everything uses one credit balance, shared by your workspace. See [Plans and pricing](/plans-and-account/plans-and-pricing).
