## Sign up

1. Go to [app.usevelo.ai](https://app.usevelo.ai) and select sign up.
2. Sign up with Google, or enter your email and a password.
3. If you use email, open the verification email from Velo and follow the link.

<Note>
Sign up with your work email to get **2×** the credits on the Free plan. Work-email workspaces also keep the Brand Kit on Free.
</Note>

**Password rules:**  A phone number is optional.

## Set up your workspace

After you sign up, Velo asks you to set up your workspace. A workspace is where your team's videos, Brand Kit and credits live.

1. Enter a name for your workspace.
2. Add a logo, if you'd like. You can add one later.
3. Answer **How did you hear about us?**
4. Continue to the home screen.

<Warning>
On the workspace setup screen, **Go back** signs you out. Finish setup first, and change anything later in **Settings**.
</Warning>

## FAQs

**Can I sign up with Google?**

Yes. Select Google on the sign-up screen and choose your account.

**Why should I use my work email?**

Work emails get **2×** the credits on Free, and their workspaces keep the Brand Kit on Free.

**I didn't get the verification email. What do I do?**

Check your spam folder, then ask for a new email from the sign-in screen.
