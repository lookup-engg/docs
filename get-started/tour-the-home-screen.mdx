## Chat box

Type what you want and send it. The Velo Agent takes it from there.

- **Attach:** Add files to your request, up to **10** per message (video, audio, images, PDFs, Office files, text and fonts).
- **Enhance prompt:** Velo rewrites a short request into a fuller one. Check it before you send.
- **Send:** Starts the chat.

## Six creation options

Below the chat box are six options: **Record Yourself**, **Generate with Avatar**, **Record Screen**, **Turn PDF to video**, **Polish a recording** and **Turn URL to video**. Each one starts a chat set up for that kind of material. See [Choose how to start](/create/choose-how-to-start).

## Templates

The template gallery sits under the chat box. **From your team** shows your workspace's own templates, and **Start with a template** shows Velo's.

## Sidebar

- **Recent chats:** Every video starts as a chat. Find, rename, pin or delete chats here.
- **My videos:** All your videos, including ones still in progress.
- **Libraries, Templates and Assets:** Shared places for videos, video formats and files.
- **Brand Kit, Voice clone and Face clone:** Set up your brand and your presenter.
- **Connectors:** Knowledge bases, Velo MCP, Slack, MCP servers and Applications.

Settings open from the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar.

## FAQs

**Where are my settings?**

In the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar. Select it, then select **Settings**.

**What's the Get Started checklist?**

A short list on the home screen that helps you set up a Brand Kit, clones and a knowledge base. See [Set up your workspace](/get-started/set-up-your-workspace).

**Does Enhance prompt always help?**

No. It can add things you didn't ask for, such as screen recordings for a PDF request. Read the result before you send it.
