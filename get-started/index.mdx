## FAQs

**How long does a first video take?**

A few minutes for most videos. You can leave the page while Velo works.

**Do I need to install anything?**

No, except to record your screen. That needs the [Velo Companion extension](/create/velo-companion-extension).

**Is there a free plan?**

Yes. Free includes public voices and avatars. See [Plans and pricing](/plans-and-account/plans-and-pricing).
