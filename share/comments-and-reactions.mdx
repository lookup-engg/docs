## Leave a comment

1. Open the video's share page.
2. Pause at the moment you want to comment on.
3. Type your comment, up to **200** characters.
4. Choose **Everyone** or **Team only**, then post it.

Comments are tied to the moment in the video.

## Reactions

Select a reaction while you watch. Reactions show on the share page.

## Who sees what

- **Everyone:** Anyone who can open the share page.
- **Team only:** Members of your workspace only.

## FAQs

**Can viewers comment without a Velo account?**

  Yes, on the share page.

**Can I turn viewer feedback into edits?**

Yes. Copy the comment into the video's chat, or comment in the AI Editor. See [Edit by chat and comments](/edit/edit-by-chat-and-comments).

**Are team-only comments ever public?**

No. Only your workspace's members see them, including in the Velo Public Library.
