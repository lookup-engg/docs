## Set a thumbnail

1. Open **Thumbnail settings**:
   - In the AI Editor, select **Share** → **Thumbnail settings**.
   - Or, in **Libraries** or **My videos**, open the video card's **⋯** menu and select **Thumbnail settings**.
2. Choose **Video frame** and scrub to a moment. Or choose **Custom thumbnail** and upload a PNG or JPEG up to **5 MB**.
3. Check the preview, then confirm.

To go back to the automatic image, select **Reset to default**.

## Good to know

- **Where it shows:** On Velo video cards and on the share page.
- **Cropping:** Images with a different shape are center-cropped to fit. The preview shows the crop.
- **It stays:** Your thumbnail stays when you export the video again.
- **Who can change it:** Anyone in your workspace.

## FAQs

**Why can't I pick a frame?**

The video needs to render first. You'll see **Render the video before choosing a thumbnail frame.** Export the video, then try again.

**Does it change the image in link previews on social sites?**

No. The thumbnail shows in Velo and on the share page.

**What size should my image be?**

Use the same shape as your video, up to **5 MB**. Other shapes are center-cropped.
