## Add a passcode

1. Open the video and select **Export**, then open the **Share Velo** panel.
2. Turn on **Passcode**.
3. Copy the passcode and send it with the link.

Velo creates a passcode of at least **8** characters.

## Require an email

1. In the **Share Velo** panel, turn on **Email required to watch**.
2. Send the link.

Viewers enter their email before the video plays. Their emails then show in [Track views](/share/track-views).

## FAQs

**Can I use a passcode and an email together?**

  Yes. Turn on both.

**Does a viewer need a Velo account?**

No. They need only the link, plus the passcode or their email if you turned them on.

**How do I share many videos behind one sign-in?**

Use the Velo Public Library. See [Velo Public Library](/organize/velo-public-library).
