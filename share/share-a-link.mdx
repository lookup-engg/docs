## Get a link

1. In the header, select **Export**.
2. Select **Get a sharable link**. Velo renders the video.
3. When the render finishes, copy the link.

Rendering can take a few minutes for longer videos. Velo emails you when it's done.

## Share options

The **Share Velo** panel has these options:

- **Sharing:** Turn the link on or off. With it off, the link stops working.
- **Passcode:** Ask viewers for a passcode. See [Secure links](/share/secure-links).
- **Email required to watch:** Ask viewers for their email. See [Secure links](/share/secure-links).
- **Download MP4:** Save the video as a file. See [Download an MP4](/share/download-an-mp4).

To put the video in a library, use **Add to library**. See [Libraries](/organize/libraries).

## What viewers see

The share page plays your video, with reactions and timestamped comments. See [Comments and reactions](/share/comments-and-reactions).

## FAQs

**Do share links have a watermark on Free?**

No. Only downloaded MP4s on Free carry a watermark.

**Does the link change when I edit the video?**

  Export again after editing so the link shows your latest version.

**Can I stop sharing a link?**

Yes. Turn off **Sharing** in the **Share Velo** panel.
