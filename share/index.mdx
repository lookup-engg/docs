## FAQs

**Do share links have a watermark?**

No. Only downloaded MP4s on Free carry a watermark.

**Can I see who watched?**

Yes. Turn on **Email required to watch** to see viewers by email. See [Track views](/share/track-views).

**Do viewers need a Velo account?**

No.
