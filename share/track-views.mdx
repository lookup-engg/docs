## Open your analytics

1. In **My videos**, open the video card's menu.
2. Select the analytics option.
3. Read the viewer table.

## The viewer table

- **Visitor:** Who watched. With **Email required to watch** on, this shows each viewer's email.
- **When:** When they watched.
- **% watched:** How much of the video they saw.

## Export your analytics

Select **Export analytics** to download the table. It appears once your video has viewers.

<Tip>
To see viewers by name, turn on **Email required to watch** before you share. See [Secure links](/share/secure-links).
</Tip>

## FAQs

**Why don't I see viewer emails?**

The link didn't require an email. Turn on **Email required to watch** for future viewers.

**Why is Export analytics missing?**

It appears once the video has at least one viewer.

**Can I hide the view count from viewers?**

  Yes. The creator can turn off view counts.
