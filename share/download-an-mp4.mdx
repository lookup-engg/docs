## Download your video

1. In the header, select **Export**.
2. In the **Share Velo** panel, select **Download MP4**.
3. Save the file.

If the video hasn't rendered yet, Velo renders it first.

## FAQs

**Why does my download have a watermark?**

Downloads on the Free plan carry a watermark. Share links don't. Upgrade to remove it from downloads.

**Can I download an older version?**

Yes. Select it in **Version history** and render it. See [Version history](/edit/version-history).

**Can I download from Slack?**

Yes. Ask @Velo for a download link. See [Velo Slack Agent](/connect/slack).
