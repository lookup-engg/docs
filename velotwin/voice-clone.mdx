<Note>
  **VeloTwin** is your AI presenter: a **voice clone** and a **face clone**. In the app they are set up separately — **Voice clone** and **Face clone** in the sidebar — and combined when you add a VeloTwin to a video.
</Note>

Voice cloning allows you to generate high-quality AI narration that sounds exactly like you. By recording a short script, you can create a personalised digital voice to use across all your video projects.

## Accessing the Voice Library

**Step 1:** Select **Voice clone** from the left sidebar navigation menu.

**Step 2:** Review the available options, organized into three sections:

- **Your Voices** — custom voice clones you've created
- **Workspace Wide Voices** — a voice one of your teammates has promoted for the whole workspace to use (see below)
- **Public Voices** — a library of ready-to-use voices available to everyone

## Sharing a voice with your workspace

Any custom voice you create can be promoted to **Workspace Wide** so every member of the workspace can use it — useful for a consistent narrator across a team's videos. Once promoted, it appears under Workspace Wide Voices for all members.

## Setting Up a New Clone

**Step 1:** Click **\+ Create new** in the Your Voices section.

**Step 2:** Enter a name for your voice clone in the text field (for example, "Andy") and click **Continue**.

## Recording the Calibration Script

**Step 1:** Choose your preferred microphone from the dropdown menu.

**Step 2:** Click **Start recording** and read the provided script aloud.

**Step 3:** Speak at a steady pace and maintain a relaxed, natural tone as you would in a normal conversation. This helps the system capture your actual sound so the clone feels natural.

**Step 4:** Click **Finish recording** once you have completed the script.

## Processing and Verification

After you finish recording, Velo will analyze the audio to clean up the sound and create your clone. If the system couldn't capture your voice cleanly - often due to background noise or missed words - you will see a "Couldn't capture your voice" notification. In this case, select **Re-record** to provide a clearer sample.

Once the analysis is complete, your new voice clone will appear in your custom library and is ready for use in any future video projects.
