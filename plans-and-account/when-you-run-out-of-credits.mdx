**Plans:** Free, Pro, Ultra · **Uses credits:** No

Running low is fixable, and your work is safe. Here's what happens and how to continue.

## Why the agent stops

- **Each turn needs credits to start:** Before the agent starts a turn, your workspace needs a minimum credit balance.
- **The agent can stop mid-turn:** If the balance drops below that minimum while the agent works, it stops. You'll see **Stopped. You've reached the usage limit**.
- **Your work so far is kept:** Stopping doesn't undo anything the agent has already made.
- **The balance is shared:** Credits belong to your workspace, so a teammate's work also uses them.

When you're out, the chat shows **You're out of usage credits** with an **Upgrade Plan** button.

## Continue after running out

1. Add credits:
   - On Pro or Ultra, the owner can [buy a top-up](/plans-and-account/top-ups-invoices-and-payment).
   - On any plan, the owner can [upgrade the plan](/plans-and-account/plans-and-pricing).
   - Or wait until your plan's credits refresh at the start of your next billing cycle.
2. Go back to the chat.
3. Ask the agent to continue (for example, "Carry on from where you stopped").

```text
Carry on from where you stopped. Keep everything you've already made.
```

## Check your balance and usage

- **Workspace usage:** **Settings** → **Usage** shows your workspace's balance, what's reserved for renders in progress, and what's been used this cycle.
- **Your own usage:** **Settings** → **My usage** shows what each of your actions used.
- **Reserved credits:** While a video renders, Velo holds credits as **Reserved** until the render finishes.

## FAQs

**Did I lose my video when the agent stopped?**

No. Everything the agent made before it stopped is kept. Add credits and ask it to continue.

**Why does the chat say I'm out when my balance isn't zero?**

Each turn needs a minimum balance to start. A small balance can be below that minimum.

**Can I see what a video will cost before I start?**

No. Credit use depends on how complex each request is. Check **Settings** → **My usage** afterward to see what each action used.
