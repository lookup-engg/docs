**Plans:** Free, Pro, Ultra, Enterprise · **Role:** Owner (to change the plan) · **Uses credits:** No

Pick the plan that fits your team, and see how your workspace's credits are used.

## How credits work

- **One balance for everything:** Since Sep 1, 2026, everything you make in Velo uses one credit balance. There are no separate pools for recordings and prompts.
- **The balance belongs to the workspace:** Everyone in your workspace shares the same credits.
- **Monthly credits reset:** Your plan's credits refresh each billing cycle. Credits left over at renewal expire.
- **Top-ups roll over:** Credits you buy as a top-up stay until you use them. See [Top-ups, invoices and payment](/plans-and-account/top-ups-invoices-and-payment).
- **Cost depends on the request:** Credit use varies with how complex each request is. The same task can cost a different amount each time.

## Plans

For current prices, credits and seats, see [usevelo.ai/pricing](https://usevelo.ai/pricing).

| Plan | Best for | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Trying Velo with your own product, docs or recordings | Public voices and avatars. No voice clone, custom face or top-ups. Downloaded MP4s carry a watermark. Sign up with a work email for more credits. |
| **Pro** | One person making videos every week | Voice and face clones, Brand Kit, top-ups. |
| **Ultra** | Teams and power users | More credits and seats than Pro, plus everything in Pro. |
| **Enterprise** | Companies rolling Velo out across teams | A custom plan with custom credits and seats, arranged with Velo's team. [Book a demo](https://usevelo.ai). |

**Brand Kit on Free:** Workspaces created with a work email keep the Brand Kit on Free. Workspaces created with a personal email need Pro or Ultra.

## Change your plan

Only the workspace owner can change the plan.

1. Open the workspace menu, then select **Settings**.
2. Under **Workspace**, select **Plans & Billing**.
3. Select the plan you want and confirm.

What happens next: the new plan applies to your whole workspace. If you cancel, your workspace returns to Free and keeps any top-up credits.

## FAQs

**What uses credits?**

Most work Velo does for you uses credits. That includes:

- Every message to the agent, including answering its questions and approving a script
- Edits: chat requests, timestamped comments and quick actions such as **Change voice**
- Avatar video
- Agentic Recording, for the whole time it runs
- Rendering a video or a share link
- Converting a video to a template
- Voice-overs and voice clones
- Requests in Slack before a video exists

To see what each action used, open **Settings** → **My usage**. Usage names include **AI Agent Usage**, **Voice-over Usage**, **Voice Clone Usage**, **Avatar Video Usage** and **Velo in Slack**.

**Do my unused credits carry over?**

No, not your monthly plan credits. They expire when your plan renews. Top-up credits roll over until you use them.

**Who can see and use our credits?**

Everyone in your workspace. Credits belong to the workspace, and any member can see the balance and usage.

**Do you offer Enterprise plans?**

Yes. Enterprise is a custom plan for companies bringing Velo to many teams. [Book a demo](https://usevelo.ai) to set it up.

**Can we send every prospect a video that greets them by name?**

Yes, on Enterprise. Record one video with your own avatar and voice, and Velo makes a personal copy for each contact in HubSpot. Book a demo to see it built on one of your own videos.
