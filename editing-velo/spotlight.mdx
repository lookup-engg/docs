---
title: "Spotlight"
description: "Dim the screen around the area you want viewers to focus on."
---

Spotlight dims everything around a region of the screen, pulling attention to one part of the recording without cutting away from the rest. Use it to point at a button, a field, or a section while the rest of the interface stays visible.

Unlike [Callout](/editing-velo/callout), Spotlight does not zoom — it only dims.

## Adding a spotlight

**Step 1:** Open the **Insert** panel from the right sidebar.

**Step 2:** Under **Effects**, select **Spotlight**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/editing-velo/spotlight/01.png" alt="Insert panel with Callout and Spotlight under Effects" />
</Frame>

**Step 3:** A **Spotlight Region** appears on the canvas and a block is added to the **Spotlight** track on the timeline. Drag the region over the area you want to highlight, and drag its handles to resize.

## Spotlight settings

Selecting the region opens **Spotlight Settings** in the right-hand panel.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/editing-velo/spotlight/02.png" alt="Spotlight Settings with background opacity, colour, border radius, and duration" />
</Frame>

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Background Opacity** | How dark the surrounding area becomes. Higher makes the spotlight stand out more |
| **Background Color** | The colour of the dimmed area. Defaults to `#000000` |
| **Border Radius** | Rounds the corners of the spotlight region, from a sharp rectangle to a pill |
| **Duration** | How long the effect stays active |

Each control has its own **Reset** button. **Close Spotlight editor** returns to the Screen panel.

## Timing

The block on the **Spotlight** track shows the current opacity — for example, `Spotlight 70%`. Drag its edges to change when the effect starts and ends, or use the **Duration** slider.
