---
title: "Callout"
description: "Draw a highlighted box around part of the screen, with a zoom to match."
---

Callout draws a highlighted box around a region of the screen so viewers see exactly where to look. Unlike [Spotlight](/editing-velo/spotlight), which only dims the surroundings, Callout also zooms toward the region.

## Adding a callout

**Step 1:** Open the **Insert** panel from the right sidebar.

**Step 2:** Under **Effects**, select **Callout**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/editing-velo/callout/01.png" alt="Insert panel with Callout and Spotlight under Effects" />
</Frame>

**Step 3:** A **Callout Region** appears on the canvas and a block is added to the **Callout** track. Drag and resize it to frame what you want to highlight.

## Callout settings

Selecting the region opens **Callout Settings** in the right-hand panel.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/editing-velo/callout/02.png" alt="Callout Settings with scale, background opacity, colour, border radius, and duration" />
</Frame>

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Scale** | How far the callout zooms in. This is separate from dragging the region — it sets the zoom, not the frame |
| **Background Opacity** | How solid the fill inside the region is. Lower values leave only the border visible |
| **Background Color** | The fill colour. Defaults to `#000000` |
| **Border Radius** | Rounds the corners of the region |
| **Duration** | How long the callout stays visible |

Each control has its own **Reset** button. **Close Callout editor** returns to the Screen panel.

## Timing

The block on the **Callout** track shows the current scale — for example, `Callout 1.3x`. Drag its edges to adjust when it appears, or use the **Duration** slider.
