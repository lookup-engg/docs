When Velo records a website for you from a pasted link, it draws the cursor
itself rather than capturing a real one. That means you can restyle it
afterwards. The **Cursor** panel in the right sidebar is where you do it.

<Note>
  The Cursor panel appears for videos made with [Paste a link](/creating-a-velo/paste-a-link).
  Screen recordings and uploaded videos already contain a real cursor, so there
  is nothing to restyle and the panel is not shown.
</Note>

## Hiding the cursor

Turn on **Hide cursor** at the top of the panel to remove the pointer from the
video completely. Everything below it stops applying.

## Cursor style and size

**Cursor Style** offers eight pointers — arrows in several weights, a hand, a
grab, and higher-contrast variants. Click one to swap it in; the preview
updates immediately.

**Cursor Size** scales the pointer up or down. Larger sizes read better when
the finished video will be watched on a phone or embedded small.

## Hiding an idle cursor

**Hide cursor if not moving** fades the pointer out when nothing is happening,
so a stationary arrow doesn't sit on top of the thing you're explaining.
**Hide not moving cursor after** sets how many seconds of stillness pass before
it disappears.

## Click sound

**Click Sound** adds an audio cue every time the cursor clicks. Three options:

| Option | What it sounds like |
| --- | --- |
| **None** | silent — clicks are visible but not audible |
| **Magic Mouse™** | a light, high tap |
| **MX Master™** | a deeper mechanical click |

**Click Sound Volume** balances it against the narration. Keep it low; the
click is a cue, not a beat.

## Rotation

Open **Rotation** and turn on **Smart Rotate** to have the pointer tilt slightly
in the direction it's travelling, the way a cursor does when it's chasing
something. The slider sets how far it tilts.
