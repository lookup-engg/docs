Velo has four plans. Every plan gives you the full editor and lets you share videos without watermarks. You can start for free — no credit card required.

Prices and inclusions below match the [Velo pricing page](https://usevelo.ai/pricing/).

## Plans

### Free — \$0/month

Best for exploring Velo.

- **15 Velo minutes** per month
- **500 Prompt to Video credits** per month
- **3 seats**
- **1 voice clone**
- **1 avatar video** per month
- Full access to the editor
- Prompt to video, document to video, and screen recording
- Share videos without watermarks
- [Video analytics](/creating-a-velo/video-analytics) on every video
- Downloads are watermarked by default
- Basic support

---

### Pro — \$49/month

Best value for most users.

Everything in Free, plus:

- **60 Velo minutes** per month
- **2,000 Prompt to Video credits** per month
- **5 seats**
- **3 voice clones**
- **1 avatar**
- No watermarks on downloads
- Edit narration
- [Multilingual output](/editing-velo/translations)
- Secure video links
- **3 script retries** per video
- **3 voice regenerations** per video
- **5 link-to-video** conversions per month

---

### Ultra — \$200/month (\$167/month billed yearly)

Built for high-volume output.

Everything in Pro, plus:

- **600 Velo minutes** per month
- **8,000 Prompt to Video credits** per month
- **20 seats** included
- **Unlimited voice clones**
- **3 avatars**
- **30 minutes** of avatar video per month
- Custom [VeloTwins](/velotwin/voice-clone)
- Shared [workspace](/workspaces/overview)
- Team video [library](/workspaces/libraries)
- [Brand Kit](/workspaces/brand-kit) and brand consistency controls
- Auto-updating demos
- **Unlimited link-to-video**
- **10 script retries** per video
- **10 voice regenerations** per video
- Priority rendering

---

### Enterprise — custom

For teams that need governance, security, and scale.

Everything in Ultra, plus:

- Custom Velo minutes, seats, avatars, and voice clones
- SSO and SAML, SCIM, RBAC, advanced access controls
- Content governance, shared libraries, private and public share pages
- Data residency, SCORM export
- API and SDK, custom integrations
- Workflow-triggered videos, agent-generated videos, knowledge base integrations
- Custom onboarding, a customer success manager, security review support
- SLA and priority rendering

## What are Velo minutes?

Velo minutes are the unit of usage for recording-based videos. Each plan includes a monthly allowance shared across everyone in the workspace, consumed based on the length of the recordings and videos you generate. Your allowance resets at the start of every billing cycle.

## Prompt to Video credits

[Prompt to Video](/prompt-to-video/overview) is billed separately from Velo minutes, from its own credit pool: **500 credits/month on Free, 2,000 on Pro, and 8,000 on Ultra**. See [Prompt to Video billing & credits](/prompt-to-video/billing-credits) for the full breakdown and top-up options.

<Note>
  Some screens in the app label this pool **Reach credits**. It is the same pool.
</Note>

## Seats

Seats determine how many people can belong to a workspace.

| Plan | Seats |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 3 |
| Pro | 5 |
| Ultra | 20 |
| Enterprise | Custom |

The **Members** page in Settings shows your current count — for example, "19/20 Seats Available" — with a **Contact us** link if you need more than your plan includes.

## Top-up

If you run out before your cycle resets, buy more from **Settings → Plans & Billing → Top-up**. Velo minutes and Prompt to Video credits are topped up separately, under their own sub-tabs. See [Plans & billing](/settings/plans-billing).

## Yearly billing

Ultra is **\$167/month billed yearly**, against \$200/month billed monthly. Switch between monthly and yearly on the [pricing page](https://usevelo.ai/pricing/).

## Questions?

If you have questions about which plan is right for you, reach out via the chat widget in the app.
